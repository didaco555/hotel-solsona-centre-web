The area

Solsona and surroundings

A medieval town and capital of the Solsonès region. The cathedral, the Diocesan Museum and the old town are a few minutes' walk from the hotel.

A base for the Port del Comte ski resort, hiking and mountain-bike routes, the Solsona Carnival and local wineries.

  • Old town and cathedral 5 min walk
  • Llosa del Cavall reservoir 18 km
  • Cardona Castle 20 km
  • Port del Comte ski resort 30 km
  • Barcelona 1 h 45 min
FAQ

Practical information

What are the benefits of booking directly?

Booking platforms such as Booking.com charge the hotel a commission for their service. Direct bookings by phone or WhatsApp carry no commission, which allows us to offer a better rate.

What are the phone line hours?

Phone line open until 9 pm. Outside those hours, contact us on WhatsApp at +34 650 464 032.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 1 pm and check-out until 11 am. Late arrivals can be arranged with the hotel.

Is there parking?

There is free parking very close to the hotel.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are not allowed.

Are groups accepted?

Yes. The hotel has separate rooms for groups. Group rates available on request.
